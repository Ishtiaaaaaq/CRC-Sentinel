How the check works

The backup data is processed again with the same generator polynomial. A zero verification remainder means the transmitted CRC is consistent with the received data; a non-zero remainder indicates a detected error.

CONCEPT

CRC Operating Principle

01

Append zeros

The data is extended by the degree of the generator polynomial before division.

02

Modulo-2 division

Binary division is performed using XOR instead of arithmetic subtraction.

03

CRC remainder

The final remainder becomes the CRC value and is appended to form the codeword.

04

Verify at receiver

The received codeword is divided again. A zero remainder means no error is detected.
